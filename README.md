# Roast Your Fridge — One Line, One Laugh

One-line roast of a fridge: it hums all night like it pays rent, yet the coldest thing inside is a jar of pickles older than my last relationship, lit by a bulb that judges me at 2 a.m. Built for the make.fun bounty "One Line, One Laugh: Roast Your Fridge" — a single line, a specific detail, and a concrete image to close it.
